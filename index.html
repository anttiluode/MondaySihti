!
The observer's most important limitation

The image has 64 hidden cells but only 56 scalar time measurements, so full-state identification is impossible even with noiseless data. The cable has seven measurements for seven compartments, but the inversion is ill-conditioned. With noise, theoretical rank is not practical recoverability. Compressing those measurements cannot manufacture additional information.

THE PROTOCOL

Nothing up the sleeve.

Five deterministic seeds. Each seed: 400 training states, 140 validation states, 180 independently held-out test states. A linear ridge decoder is trained on synthetic hidden-state labels; temporal band selection and regularization are tuned on validation only. An image uses eight spatial sensors; the cable exposes one soma sensor. Sensor noise is independent before applying the residue transform.

Fairness boundary: all seven checkpoints were sampled first; the three-band restriction is a storage budget, not a limit of three physical measurements. The PCA encoder learns from training examples; raw and residue approaches search the same 35 three-time combinations using validation. Full-history ridge variants are shown for reference, and may differ because regularization is not invariant to coordinate changes.

This is an exploratory synthetic experiment with a fixed linear decoder family. It does not prove a universal impossibility of residue-based gains, address nonlinear dendritic memories, or recover QKV or real neural sources.
